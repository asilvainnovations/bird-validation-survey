## 1. Introduction

### What is BIRD 2026-2035?
The **Bangsamoro Investment Roadmap (BIRD) 2026-2035** is the comprehensive, evidence-based strategic plan designed to transform the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM) into a competitive, inclusive, and sustainable investment destination. Developed by the Board of Investments - Ministry of Trade, Investments and Tourism (BOI-MTIT BARMM), BIRD serves as the region's 10-year master plan.

The roadmap is structured around the **BEIE Framework** (Bangsamoro Economic and Investment Ecosystem), organizing investment clusters into **Foundations, Transformers, Enablers, Connectors,** and **Financiers**, all anchored by **Moral Governance** as the central operating system.

### Why Your Feedback Matters
Your participation ensures that the BIRD 2026-2035 reflects ground-level realities, diverse provincial perspectives, and technical rigor. Your feedback directly shapes policy priorities, resource allocation, and implementation sequencing over the next decade.

### Anonymity and Data Protection
The survey is designed to be compliant with the **Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)**.
* **Voluntary Participation:** All questions are optional unless explicitly marked as required.
* **Anonymity Option:** You may complete the survey without providing Personally Identifiable Information (PII) like your name or email.
* **Two separate consents:** Consenting to *participate* is not the same as consenting to have your (de-identified) answers appear in the public live dashboard — you're asked about each separately, and the second is entirely your choice.
* **Secure Storage:** Data is transmitted via HTTPS and stored in a Supabase PostgreSQL database with Row-Level Security (RLS). Submissions are also rate-limited to protect the instrument against automated spam.
* **Privacy Policy:** available from the footer link in the app (Privacy Policy), and via [the BIRD platform's hosted copy](https://asilvainnovations.github.io/BIRD-2026-2035/public/privacy-policy.html).

### Optional Sign-In
You do **not** need an account to take the survey — it's fully anonymous-friendly by default. An optional "Sign In" is available in the header if you'd like your profile details remembered for a future visit; it has no bearing on whether your responses count or how they're used.

### The BIRD AI Assistant
A floating assistant icon is available throughout the survey (bottom corner of the screen) if you'd like a plain-language explanation of a framework term, cluster, or archetype while you're answering. It's optional and never required to complete a question.

## 2. Before You Start

### System Requirements
* **Browser:** Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, Edge 90+, Safari 14+, or Opera 76+
* **Screen:** 1024x768 minimum (1366x768+ recommended for optimal chart/image viewing)
* **Connection:** Stable broadband (3 Mbps+ recommended for multimedia and lazy-loaded images)

### Estimated Completion Time
* **Full survey:** 45–60 minutes
* **Partial survey:** 20–30 minutes
* **Save & Resume:** Supported automatically via your browser's local storage. Your progress is saved on this device every time you navigate between steps — it is not synced to any account, so resuming requires the same browser and device you started on.

## 3. Survey Navigation

### How the Wizard Works
The survey uses a 16-step wizard interface (Steps 0–15). You progress sequentially, but the interface allows flexible navigation.

### Progress Indicator & Step Jumper
A sticky progress bar at the top of the screen shows your completion percentage. The **Step Jumper** (the row of step labels below the progress bar) allows you to click any step to navigate directly to it without losing your saved progress.

### Offline Resilience
If your internet connection drops while submitting, your responses remain saved locally on this device. When connectivity is restored, retry submission from where you left off — nothing you've entered is lost in the meantime.

## 5. Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 0: Welcome & Orientation
**Purpose:** Introduce the survey and foundational systems thinking concepts.
**What to do:** Watch the introductory video on Systems Thinking. Review the Causal Loop Diagram (CLD) and Systems Archetypes reference images. Answer quick-start questions about your readiness and understanding of the ecosystem.

### Step 1: Privacy & Consent
**Purpose:** Legal compliance and data protection.
**What to do:** Read the privacy notice and check the consent boxes. *Note: along with the final consent in Step 15, this is the only strictly required section to proceed.*

### Step 2: Your Profile
**Purpose:** Segmentation for robust, multi-dimensional analysis.
**What to do:** Select your primary province of engagement, stakeholder category, and areas of expertise. Your name, email, and organization are optional but helpful for follow-up. A network-accuracy question lets you rate how well a visualization of BARMM stakeholder relationships matches reality.

### Step 3: BEIE & Systems Thinking
**Purpose:** Validate the overall framework and two core causal-loop dynamics.
**What to do:** Rate your understanding of the BEIE framework, then validate the **Investment-Development Virtuous Cycle** and the **Governance-Investor Confidence Loop** — two causal loop diagrams, each with an accuracy rating and an optional follow-up.

### Steps 4–9: Cluster Assessments & Systems Archetypes
**Purpose:** Validate SWOT factors and systems dynamics across the BEIE framework's five clusters plus its operating systems.
**What to do:** For each cluster, you rate specific strategic factors using paired **Impact (1-5)** and **Likelihood (1-5)** scales, and see a live-computed score as you go. You'll also validate one systems archetype per cluster (e.g. "Tragedy of the Commons" in Step 4, "Shifting the Burden" in Step 8) by selecting how accurately it describes BARMM's reality, with an optional open-text follow-up. Step 9 includes an additional 1–5 rating question about how much Moral Governance de-risks capital.

### Steps 10–13: Strategy, Metrics & Budget
**Purpose:** Validate the IEDS strategy, KPI frameworks, and budget phases.
**What to do:** Rate the clarity and feasibility of the 3-phase execution plan, score strategic options using the 7-criteria IEDS matrix, assess KPI importance (Step 11 also includes the "Drifting Goals" archetype), and evaluate budget realism and risk concerns.

### Steps 14–15: Resources & Submission
**Purpose:** Finalize engagement preferences and submit.
**What to do:** Indicate your preferred engagement format (e.g., workshops, interviews), review your response summary, confirm data accuracy, check the final consent box, and click **Submit Survey**.

### 6.1 SurveyWizard.tsx & State Management
The wizard uses React `useState` hooks for each section. Field names are meant to match `SurveySchemaType` (via Zod, generated from `src/lib/swot-content.ts`) exactly, so the frontend and backend never drift apart on what a given question is called — this is actively checked as part of the project's audit process rather than assumed.

### 6.2 formulas.ts (Real-Time Scoring)

| Function | Metric | Formula |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `calculateStrengthRI` | Strength Resilience Index | `(Impact × Likelihood) / 5` |
| `calculateOpportunityRI` | Opportunity Resilience Index | `√(Impact × Likelihood)` |
| `calculateWeaknessRisk` | Weakness Risk Level | `Impact × Likelihood` |
| `calculateThreatVI` | Threat Vulnerability Index | `(Impact² × Likelihood) / 25` |

### 6.3 api.ts & Edge Function Submission
The `submitSurvey()` function in `src/lib/api.ts` handles persistence via the **`survey-submit` Supabase Edge Function**, with a 20-second timeout so a stalled connection doesn't leave you staring at a spinner indefinitely. The Edge Function independently re-validates your consent answer (it does not simply trust whatever the app sends), applies a submission rate limit, wraps the flat payload into a `response_data` JSONB column, and extracts key filtering fields (`demo_province`, `demo_category`) for dashboard performance.

```typescript
// Simplified flow of src/lib/api.ts
export async function submitSurvey(data: Partial<SurveySchemaType>) {
  const payload = {
    ...data,
    consent_final: data.q1_consent_participate === true, // derived, never trusted as pre-set
  };
  const response = await fetch(EDGE_FUNCTIONS.SUBMIT_SURVEY, {
    method: "POST",
    headers: getEdgeFunctionHeaders(),
    body: JSON.stringify(payload),
    signal: AbortSignal.timeout(20_000),
  });
  const result = await response.json();
  if (!response.ok) throw new Error(result.error);
  return result; // { success: true, id }
}
```

## 7. Question Types Explained

### SWOT Factor Scales (Impact × Likelihood)
Used in Steps 4–9. You provide two independent ratings (1-5) for each factor. The combined score determines strategic priority, and updates live as you answer.
* **Impact (1-5):** How big of an effect does this factor have? (1 = Very small, 5 = Very large)
* **Likelihood (1-5):** How likely is this factor to persist or happen? (1 = Very unlikely, 5 = Very likely)

### Systems Archetype & Causal Loop Validation
Appears in Steps 3, 4–9, and 11 — not only in the cluster sections. You select how accurately a causal loop model or systems archetype describes BARMM's reality:
* **Very accurately**
* **Somewhat accurately**
* **Needs revision**
* **Not accurate**

One question (Step 9, on Moral Governance de-risking capital) uses a 1–5 rating scale instead of these four options — it's aggregated differently on the backend (a rating of 4 or 5 counts toward consensus) precisely because it's a different question type, not an oversight.

### Rating Scales & Open Text
Standard 1-5 Likert scales are used for KPI, BSC, and feasibility validation. Open text fields are used for qualitative feedback, archetype follow-ups, and additional comments.

## 8. BIRD Score Interpretation

The **BIRD Live Score Panel** updates in real-time as you rate SWOT factors in Steps 4–9 (Step 3 has causal-loop validation questions but no Impact × Likelihood SWOT scales, so it doesn't feed this panel).

### Score Range Interpretations (Normalized 1-5 Scale)
*Applies to Strength RI, Opportunity RI, and Threat VI.*
| Score Range | Interpretation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **4.0 - 5.0** | **Critical Priority:** Immediate strategic attention required. |
| **3.0 - 3.9** | **High Priority:** Significant strategic relevance. |
| **2.0 - 2.9** | **Moderate:** Monitor and plan intervention. |
| **1.0 - 1.9** | **Low/Negligible:** Routine monitoring. |
*(Weakness Risk is calculated on a 1-25 scale — `Impact × Likelihood` with no normalization — where higher numbers indicate greater risk exposure. This is intentional, not an inconsistency with the other three metrics.)*

### Strategic Balance Index (SBI)
The panel synthesizes all four SWOT dimensions into a single 0-100 composite score:
`SBI = ((Strength RI + Opportunity RI) / 2) - ((Weakness Risk_norm + Threat VI) / 2) + 50`
* **> 60:** Favorable strategic position.
* **40 - 60:** Neutral/Balanced position.
* **< 40:** Challenging position requiring urgent intervention.

## 9. For Administrators

### Deployment & Monitoring
1. **Frontend Deploy:** `npm run build` → deploy `dist/` (Vercel config included in `vercel.json`; the output is a standard static SPA, so Netlify and similar hosts work too).
2. **Backend Deploy:** Apply the database migration (`supabase db push`) and deploy all five Edge Functions:
   ```bash
   supabase functions deploy survey-submit
   supabase functions deploy survey-analytics
   supabase functions deploy email-notifications
   supabase functions deploy strategic-planner-sync
   supabase functions deploy ai-strategy-assistant
   ```
3. **Monitor:** Check the `survey_response_stats` Supabase view for real-time, PII-stripped completion rates and demographic breakdowns, or call the `survey-analytics` Edge Function directly for computed BIRD scores and archetype consensus.

### Iteration Triggers
Consider revising the survey instrument or conducting targeted follow-ups if:
* Overall completion rate drops below 60%.
* A specific Systems Archetype scores a net validation of `< -0.5` (indicating strong stakeholder disagreement with the model).
* Significant demographic skew is detected (e.g., <20% representation from island provinces).

### Data Export
Access the `survey_responses` table via the Supabase Dashboard for the full record (service-role access only — RLS restricts it). Use the `survey_response_stats` view for safe, anonymized analytics; note it only includes rows where the respondent consented to both participation *and* anonymized aggregate use, so its row count will be lower than the full response total. Export to CSV/JSON for external MEL dashboard integration.

## 10. Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Survey won't load** | Clear browser cache, disable ad-blockers/script-blockers, and ensure JavaScript is enabled. If the error mentions a "dynamically imported module," a hard refresh usually resolves it — this is typically caused by the app updating between when the page loaded and when you navigated. |
| **Images not loading** | Check internet connection. Images are lazy-loaded from Supabase Storage and may take a moment on slow networks; a broken-image icon after a long wait may mean the image reference itself needs fixing on the content side rather than a network issue on yours. |
| **Lost progress** | Progress is saved in your browser's local storage. Resuming requires the same browser and device you started on — it is not synced to an account, and using a private/incognito window will not preserve it between sessions. |
| **Submission fails** | Check your network connection and try again. If you've submitted several times in quick succession, the system may briefly rate-limit further attempts as a spam-prevention measure — wait a few minutes and retry. |

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Can I skip questions?**
A: Yes. In pilot mode, all fields are optional *except* the consent checkboxes in Step 1 and the final consent confirmation in Step 15.

**Q: What happens if I close my browser?**
A: Your progress is saved locally in your browser. Reopen the survey link on the same device and browser to resume exactly where you left off.

**Q: Do I need an account or to log in?**
A: No. The survey is anonymous-friendly and requires no login or registration. An optional sign-in is available if you'd like your profile remembered for next time, but it changes nothing about how your responses are counted.

**Q: How are my responses used?**
A: They are aggregated and fed into the MEL Dashboard to shape the final BIRD 2026-2035 document. Whether your (de-identified) individual answers appear in the *public* live dashboard depends specifically on whether you consented to anonymized aggregate use in Step 1 — that's a separate choice from consenting to participate at all.

**Q: Can I change my answers after submitting?**
A: No. Once the final "Submit Survey" button is clicked, the record is finalized. If you need to make a correction, please contact the BIRD technical team.
